AI-Driven Immersive Communication Training

Research examining how immersive AR/VR environments and AI-driven simulations can help Information Technology and Cybersecurity students practice technical communication in realistic professional scenarios.

Project Overview

Practicing technical communication under pressure.

Students first researched a current security risk using standard security-risk-analysis procedures. They then entered a VirtualSpeech AR/VR environment and presented their findings to an AI-driven avatar using questions designed around professional Chief Information Officer expectations.

I managed the VirtualSpeech platform, uploaded research materials, supported classroom implementation, and assisted participants throughout the simulations. As my research role expanded, I also helped coordinate project activities and train newer undergraduate researchers.

Research Questions

Can VirtualSpeech improve communication confidence?

Can AI-driven simulations personalize and enhance classroom learning?

Can immersive environments reduce communication anxiety?

Findings & Recognition

Communication confidence, engagement, and growth

Early findings indicated that students valued the immersive experience and reported stronger confidence when practicing stressful communication scenarios. The project later expanded into additional courses and student groups.
